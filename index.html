I'm Margarette, a 21-year-old frontend designer who loves turning ideas into clean, functional interfaces — from wireframes and flowcharts for college projects and capstone work, to polished, user-friendly web layouts. I've had the chance to lead the visual design side of multiple academic projects, making sure every screen tells a clear story through thoughtful structure. I actively attend workshops focused on frontend development and UI/UX principles, constantly sharpening my skills and picking up new tools.
